Hi! I am Ankush (pronounced as Un-koo-sh), and as of 2026, a 4th year Physics PhD Candidate. I am part of [The Gopinathan Group](http://gopinathanlab.ucmerced.edu/) in the [Department of Physics](https://physics.ucmerced.edu/) at the [University of California, Merced](https://www.ucmerced.edu/). My interests lie at the theoretical and compuatational crossroads of Physics and Biology. Currently, I work on understanding (via simulations and theory) how cytotoxic T cells orchestrate intracellular transport and generate forces to move its organelles (centrosome and lytic granules) which ultimately lead to the killing of pathogenic target cells. 

Prior to this, I was part of the [Biophysics Group](https://biophysics.icts.res.in/) at the [ICTS, Bengaluru](https://www.icts.res.in/) where I worked on building a minimal model of actomyosin cytoskeleton by looking at it as an active nematic system.  

I find joy in running, playing Ultimate Frisbee, [reading books](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/64670551-ankush-gk), watching movies/shows and sketching.

I share some of my sketches and doodles at [Project Skedoodle](https://projectskedoodle.tumblr.com/).
